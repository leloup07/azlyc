01 · Approach

When existing categories no longer hold.

Digital assets are redrawing how value is owned and governed. Artificial intelligence is being adopted faster than the rules can be written. Sustainability has become a strategic constraint rather than a reporting exercise. Regulation arrives in waves — MiCA, the EU AI Act, CSRD, DLT — and each one redraws what compliance means before the previous one is absorbed.

Underneath all of this, an older question survives: what is an institution for, and what does it take to build one that endures. AZLYC was built around that question. The work sits earlier than legal advice or technical consulting — in how a board, a founder or an institution holds its structure, its technology and its culture together, with integrity, in a way that outlasts the leadership that began it.

i.

The question

Not yet sorted by discipline. A tokenization decision is also a governance decision; an AI deployment is also a question of board accountability.

ii.

The work

Architecture before implementation: decision rights, regulatory posture, custody, culture — designed as one system and handed over ready to operate.

iii.

The conviction

Better-governed institutions make the world more navigable. That is the conviction the studio operates from.

— Tokenization & Blockchain Maturity

Real-world assets, institutional infrastructure.

Tokenization of real-world assets — debt, equity, real estate, funds — as institutional infrastructure rather than experiment. We work upstream of the smart contract: issuer architecture, custody design, investor protection, regulator interface and the cross-jurisdictional posture that allows the structure to survive the next regulatory cycle.

AZLYC structured the first debt-tokenization pilot to complete the Spanish CNMV regulatory sandbox (2021–22). Active mandates in Europe and the Caucasus under MiCA, the FCA Digital Securities Sandbox and National Bank of Georgia frameworks.

AZLYC also operates Blockchain Maturity Model (BMM) assessments as a BMM-authorized Business Associate of the Government Blockchain Association (GBA). Lead assessor: certified BMM Assessor and Regional Director for Europe, GBA; Co-Chair of the UK & European Digital Asset Task Force of the Digital Asset Regulatory Authority (DARA).
